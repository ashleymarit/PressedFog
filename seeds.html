Seeds

Packets on the table.

Kraft, an ink name, a fold. What we keep in seed sits in the same light as the sheets. This page does not list prices.

Poppy

Eschscholzia californica

Coastside. Dry capsule, split by hand. The same orange as the sheet if you wait for a still day.

Yarrow

Achillea millefolium

Prairie edge. Small, grey, a little sharp. Shake the head over paper — not over the floor.

Sea kale

Crambe maritima

Strand plant. A coastal brassica in the same kraft as the others. Keep the packet dry.

Paper is the third thing on the table: rag sheets, typed slips, linen tape. We do not sell it as stationery. It is what the specimens live on.
