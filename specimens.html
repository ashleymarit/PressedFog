Specimens

Four sheets.

A folio on the table. Each pressing is the object — rag paper, linen tape, a typed slip. Labels that came with older sheets stay on the paper; the notes here are the shop’s.

Sheet 01 · fern

Pteridium aquilinum

Bracken

Latin
Pteridium aquilinum (L.) Kuhn
Found
Canyon shade, Half Moon Bay
Paper
Rag, cream, foxed. Three linen tapes.
Notes
One frond, laid once. Do not lift the sheet while it dries — the pinnae keep their plane if you leave them.

Sheet 02 · poppy

Eschscholzia californica

California poppy

Latin
Eschscholzia californica Cham.
Found
Coastside bluff, Half Moon Bay
Paper
Old herbarium stock. Tape the stems, not the petals.
Notes
Press the same day or the orange goes dull. Older labels stay on the sheet; they are part of the paper, not the shop’s address.

Sheet 03 · kelp

Macrocystis pyrifera

Giant kelp

Latin
Macrocystis pyrifera (L.) C. Agardh
Found
Tide line, Moss Beach / Pillar Point
Paper
Heavy sheet, photo corners. Salt in the fibers if you hold it.
Notes
Blades folded to fit. Rinse once, press under weight. The bladder keeps its dark even when the ribbons go olive.

Sheet 04 · yarrow

Achillea millefolium

Yarrow

Latin
Achillea millefolium L.
Found
Coastal prairie, Half Moon Bay / Montara
Paper
Cream card. Tape the stems; leave the heads free.
Notes
Umbels dry almost white. The feathery leaves take longer than the flowers. Filed with the other coastside umbels.
